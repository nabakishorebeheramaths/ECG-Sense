VALIDATION

Engineering performance stays separate from user interpretation.

MIT-BIH benchmark records are evaluated against reference annotations to calculate sensitivity, precision and F1. Uploaded ECG files without ground-truth annotations are not assigned fabricated accuracy scores.

RESPONSIBLE USE

Analysis is not diagnosis.

ECG-Sense provides digital signal-processing results and AI-assisted explanations. It does not replace professional medical evaluation.

Important: ECG-Sense is an engineering and research analysis platform. Medical interpretation should be performed by a qualified healthcare professional.
