FAQ

Common questions

What file formats are supported?

ECG-Sense currently accepts CSV and TXT text-based ECG recordings through the upload interface.

Why don't uploaded ECGs get an F1 score?

F1, sensitivity, precision, TP, FP and FN require known reference annotations. ECG-Sense does not invent those values for an arbitrary uploaded recording.

What is Gemini used for?

Gemini is used as an explanation layer for verified ECG-Sense measurements. The deterministic DSP engine remains the numerical source of truth.

Is ECG-Sense a medical diagnostic device?

No. ECG-Sense is an engineering and research analysis platform. Clinical interpretation must be performed by a qualified healthcare professional.
